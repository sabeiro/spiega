* client relationship

The most essential steps for a successful client relationship are:

- listen :: what are client needs and expectations
- stake-holding :: understand who knows, who decides and what conflicts exist between departments
- discover :: check client infrastructure and spot critical issues, often what the client describes is far from best practices
- return :: estimate the return for that project and define success metrics
- propose :: propose a service for the mentioned budget
- test :: test the progresses of your product and the delivery of the team
- tell :: keep the client updated and discuss possible adaptations
- involve :: involve the client in product tests, make sure the client won't be surprised at the time of delivery
- measure :: measure the quality of the metrics and make sure the client is satisfied
- deliver :: make sure at the delivery date there are no surprises
- update :: ask the client if everything works fine and if they need some adjustments
- upsell :: ask the client if they want to expand on other business cases capitalizing the investment for the first product
- network :: ask the client if they can promote your services to their partners of to the press

*** stake-holding

There are some tricky situations to handle:

- budget owners :: don't have the technical capabilities to understand what they need
- procurement :: they don't know what they buy but use many tricks to cut on price
- tech teams :: they don't like external providers and they feel questioned when you ask
