# video production

Viudì is a musical channel sharing ideas for musical instruments, soundtracks, music gears and DIY projects. 
Each video has an original soundtrack made with different types of instruments. Every video has a topic (mainly DIY and nature) where the tracks support the specific topic exploring multiple genres.
Each video suggests different technologies and connections between instruments, effects and loudspeakers. 
This channel is meant to motivate musicians to add devices and sounds into their music and producers to leave the audio workstation and play music.
Each track is a real time recording jam where the only edit is cut during video editing.
