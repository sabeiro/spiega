*** retail

Hobbies comes along with a passion for retail. I visit many physical stores and their products, I compare prices, ask questions and watch tons of reviews. I want to be able to judge the quality and utility of goods which helps me in many occasion to transfer these learning to devices I couldn't be judging otherwise.

- quality :: decide what is the bare minimum for the output otherwise (cameras, mics) will be completely useless
- release :: it's hard to know whether a new release is worth buying. The difference between insta360 x3 and x5 are immense and I felt I needed to upgrade. In many other cases it was not necessary

*** procurement 

I highly underestimated the effort of knowing what to buy. I often had to interrupt a work I was doing to run to a shop and buy what was missing and have less flexibility on the price. With proper planning I could buy in advance items which I thought they could be useful and which where indeed necessary later on. In that way I could save a lot in expenses.
I bought as well items I could never use which I regret buying.

- electronics :: I buy it for particular *hardware* specs but then the *software* makes it useless, I hope I can hack them one day with vibe coding
- technology :: you can always buy a more advanced gear but it's important to set a limit. For me for example was clear I never wanted 4k nor 10bit colors
