* team productivity

Success depends on team productivity and there are few things to consider to make a team functional
- requirements :: interact with product manager and client to discuss what can be done in the given time and budget
- mobilize :: make sure the assigned team has the capabilities requested, plan some of your time otherwise to fill the gaps
- briefing :: to each practitioner it should be clear the context of the work to do
- humans :: make sure people are motivated and willing to work together
- tests :: decide how to run quality tests and when to approve a feature
