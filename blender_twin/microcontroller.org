* audio applications

MCU are really useful for audio processing since they don't have the complexity of an operating system and they have low latency and high reliability.
A typical audio need is a sampling rate of 48kHz 24bit and fast effect processing. Unfortunately most of the DAC/ADC on those devices are limited 

- [[https://sensorium.github.io/Mozzi/][Mozzi]] :: arduino library
- [[https://github.com/marcel-licence][Marcel]] :: ESP32 library
- [[https://github.com/axoloti/axoloti][axoloti patcher]] :: patcher for a dsp
